## Requested behavior

The user again requested actual unlockable gun/tower models on the sides, a semi-automatic pistol, visible real ammunition instead of a gray pouch, no assisted reload in VR, and a clearer tower-defense flow.

## Implemented

- Five persistent live 3D model cards: Prism and Slow on the left; Pistol, Shotgun and Sniper on the right. They use the actual cached game geometry, not downloaded images. These are all currently playable types, not the full Tiny Unicorn Lab catalogue. Cards label unlock waves, equipment/selection, levels and prices. Pistol and Prism begin available; Shotgun/Slow unlock atwave3, Sniper atwave5. Both sides remain visible in combat. Desktop/touch cards follow the camera at screen edges; VR cards stand on either side of the starting defense position.
- Aim/click a model to choose it. Selecting a tower previews its real form on an empty selected pad; the nearby action confirms spending. The preview cannot attack. Existing built-tower actions upgrade it. Selecting an equipped gun's lower price line upgrades it between waves. Removed the old text-only tower cycle and oversized armory panel. Desktop battlefield no longer has the central mission panel blocking its view; phase/points/core, contextual actions and wave celebration remain. VR keeps the mission/wrist panel, ammo, points and handover feedback.
- Pistol fires one discrete rainbow round per fresh trigger/click/tap, with a180ms minimum interval, one of12 charges, short tracer, kick, sound and haptic pulse. Holding cannot repeat shots. It replaces the continuous beam and its unused hum oscillator. Shotgun/sniper retain their cadence and unlocks. Pistol base damage0.65 per round; weapon upgrades apply the existing multiplier.
- VR face buttons, supporting trigger, keyboard R and the hidden reload button cannot start assisted reload. In VR, pull/drop the magazine, grip the fresh one at the supporting hip, insert and release. Automatic120ms chambering still follows that physical insertion. Desktop/touch retain the one-second reload and held-trigger protection. With a missing support controller, exit VR to use fallback reload; no controller shortcut silently recalls the magazine.
- Hip supply is now a clone of the actual full rainbow magazine. It appears after removal while a magazine is not held, and hides during holding/outsideVR. Supply remains unlimited as in the established design; no new finite-ammo economy. Throws, partial-ammo reseating, gun handover, movement, snap turning and controller hands remain.
